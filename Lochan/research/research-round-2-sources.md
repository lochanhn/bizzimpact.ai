# Additional research sources and download record

Research batch 2 · November 6, 2026 · Team 33, bizzimpact.ai

**Completed scope:** 17 additional PDF publications downloaded: nine academic papers/preprints/working papers and eight policy or industry reports. Six public GitHub projects inspected through repository metadata, README, license text and selected source passages. Supplementary vendor/industry pages are listed below. Findings and interpretations are in [research-round-2-notes.md](research-round-2-notes.md).

The four PDFs from the first batch are not counted again. Downloaded does not mean fully read: review depth and PDF page locators below describe selected passages actually inspected. Bibliographies and most appendices were not read. Page numbers refer to PDF position, not necessarily printed pagination. No owner outreach, installations, application launches, paid AI calls, or live integration tests took place.
