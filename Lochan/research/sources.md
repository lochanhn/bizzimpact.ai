## S01 — Federal Reserve: 2026 Report on Employer Firms

- **Publisher/date:** Federal Reserve Banks, March 3, 2026; findings from the 2025 Small Business Credit Survey.
- **Publication page:** https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/survey/2026-report-on-employer-firms
- **Original PDF:** https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/-/media/project/clevelandfedtenant/fsbsite/reports/2026/2026-report-on-employer-firms/2026-report-on-employer-firms.pdf
- **Saved:** [Report PDF](papers/01-federal-reserve-2026-employer-firms.pdf).
- **Reviewed:** Executive summary, operational challenges, AI usage/barriers, methodology. Printed pages i–ii, 3, 26, 28, 32; PDF pages 4–5, 9, 32, 34, 38.
- **Method/limits:** 6,525 US employer firms, 1–499 employees; September 3–November 14, 2025. Weighted convenience sample, not random. AI questions were optional and have smaller denominators. Does not establish findings for sole proprietors or any one of our four sectors.
- **Why useful:** Direct evidence about business challenges and difficulties choosing/using AI.

## S02 — NFIB: Small Business and Technology, 2025

- **Authors:** Holly Wade, Peter Hansen, Madeleine Oldstone; NFIB Research Center.
- **Original PDF:** https://www.nfib.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/2025-NFIB-Technology-Survey.pdf
- **Saved:** [Report PDF](papers/02-nfib-2025-small-business-technology.pdf).
- **Reviewed:** Narrative pages 2–7 and selected question tables, including pages 14–16.
- **Related methodology article, reviewed:** https://www.nfib.com/news/analysis/nfib-survey-sheds-light-on-new-technologies-for-small-business/ — June 30, 2025.
- **Method/limits:** Article describes email invitations to 20,000 randomly selected NFIB members, March 6–31, 2025, with 521 completions. This is a membership sample, not all US firms. PDF tables have varying denominators and an industry table N=559; do not silently equate these numbers. Restaurants are only 2% of that industry table. Not a sector-specific estimate.
- **Why useful:** Technology habits, business-size differences, and reluctance to replace working systems.

## S03 — OECD: Generative AI and the SME Workforce — New Survey Evidence

- **Publisher/year:** OECD, 2025.
- **Publication/DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1787/2d08b99d-en
- **Resolved page:** https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/generative-ai-and-the-sme-workforce_2d08b99d-en.html
- **Original PDF:** https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/11/generative-ai-and-the-sme-workforce_83bafdfb/2d08b99d-en.pdf
- **Saved:** [Report PDF](papers/03-oecd-2025-generative-ai-sme-workforce.pdf).
- **Reviewed:** Publication summary; PDF methodology pages 10–11; selected barriers/training pages 43–44, 47, 49. Not the entire report.
- **Method/limits:** 5,232 firms with up to 249 employees, including one-person firms. Stratified telephone survey in seven countries, October–December 2024; US not included. Respondents unaware of generative AI were screened out of detailed interviews, with weighting adjustments for adoption estimates. Self-reported outcomes.
- **Why useful:** Explains barriers beyond tool discovery and documents how support/training is handled.

## S04 — Otis et al.: The Uneven Impact of Generative AI on Entrepreneurial Performance

- **Authors:** Nicholas G. Otis, Rowan Clarke, Solène Delecourt, David Holtz, Rembrand Koning.
- **Published article:** https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.06909 — Management Science, online July 10, 2026.
- **Resolved page:** https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.06909
- **Working-paper PDF, university-hosted:** https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/citation_file_upload/Otis_et_al_2023_UnevenImpactOfAI%20%2810%29.pdf
- **Saved:** [Working-paper PDF](papers/04-otis-et-al-uneven-impact-ai-working-paper.pdf).
- **Reviewed:** Published abstract and introduction excerpts; downloaded manuscript pages 1–3. The full 136-page manuscript/appendices have not been read.
- **Method/limits:** Randomized access to a GPT-4 assistant through WhatsApp among 640 Kenyan entrepreneurs, per the manuscript. Different country, intervention, and business population from our challenge. Published estimates differ from the older manuscript; do not mix versions.
- **Why useful:** Evidence against assuming that access to AI automatically improves business outcomes.
- **Failed access recorded:** https://www.nicholasotis.com/Res/Otis_et_al_2023_UnevenImpactOfAI.pdf — web retrieval returned 502; direct download failed name resolution. Used Columbia's copy instead.

## S05 — Published café-owner interview: Mo Shalash, The Brew Crew

- **Publisher/date:** Ristretto Time, October 16, 2026.
- **Link:** https://www.podpage.com/ristrettotime/interview-with-mo-shalash-of-the-brew-crew-in-ny/
- **Reviewed:** Episode description and transcript passages on labor, hiring, wages, and repeat customers. Audio not played; full transcript not audited.
- **Locator:** Transcript exchange on labor philosophy, followed by hiring and customer retention; retrieved text around lines 211–255.
- **Evidence type:** Named owner explaining his experience. Primary qualitative account, not a representative survey.
- **Limits:** Single café case; transcript has errors and conversational interruptions. Use paraphrases, not precise financial generalizations.
- **Why useful:** A concrete account of what an owner considers important, independent of our initial AI ideas.

## S07 — Reported landscaping interviews: labor and cost challenges

- **Title:** Pros share tips to maximize value in the face of labor and cost challenges.
- **Author/publisher:** Christina Herrick, Landscape Management.
- **Date:** July 20, 2022; updated November 3, 2023.
- **Link:** https://www.landscapemanagement.net/pros-share-tips-to-maximize-value-in-the-face-of-labor-and-cost-challenges/
- **Reviewed:** Selected reporting on Velvet Green, Left Side Lawn Care, and Emerald Lawns; “Time savers”/call handling and “Getting efficient” sections.
- **Evidence type:** Named operator accounts with concrete workflows and self-reported outcomes.
- **Limits:** Older evidence; mixes businesses of different sizes and discusses suppliers/products. Individual savings estimates are not typical-sector results or proof AI caused them.
- **Why useful:** Shows the difference between administrative bottlenecks and physical-work capacity constraints.

## S08 — HVAC missed-call Q&A: vendor perspective

- **Title:** The Hidden Cost of Missed HVAC Calls.
- **Publisher/date:** Contracting Business, Nicole Krawcke, June 22, 2026.
- **Link:** https://www.contractingbusiness.com/contracting-business-success/article/55382815/the-hidden-cost-of-missed-hvac-calls
- **Reviewed:** Opening and Q&A passages on intake, dispatch, callbacks, and conversion.
- **Interviewee:** Jason Luo, CEO of Newo.ai, an AI supplier—not an HVAC business owner.
- **Limits:** Commercial interest. Does not independently prove prevalence, revenue loss, or effectiveness of an AI receptionist. No loss estimate adopted.
- **Why retained:** Identifies a possible workflow to investigate and prevents a supplier interview being mislabeled as owner evidence.

## S09 — Restaurant costs: National Restaurant Association

- **Title/date:** Restaurant operators kept food cost ratios in check in 2024; September 10, 2025.
- **Link:** https://www.restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/restaurant-economic-insights/analysis-commentary/restaurant-operators-kept-food-cost-ratios-in-check-in-2024/
- **Reviewed:** Public article and its interpretation limits.
- **Method/limits:** Article describes underlying operating data from more than 900 US restaurant operators. Full paid Operations Data Abstract was not obtained or read. Sample is not established as representative of mom-and-pop restaurants.
- **Why useful:** Cost pressure and financial performance must be understood before suggesting more software spending.

## S10 — Restaurant technology: TouchBistro survey release

- **Title:** TouchBistro's 2025 State of Restaurants Report release.
- **Publisher/date:** TouchBistro, November 20, 2024.
- **Link:** https://www.touchbistro.com/press-releases/2025-state-of-restaurants-report-release/
- **Reviewed:** Selected release sections on automation, inventory, reservations, online ordering, and customer acquisition.
- **Evidence type:** Vendor-published survey summary for independent full-service restaurant operators.
- **Limits:** Full report/methodology not reviewed. Its very high AI-use claim should not be generalized or compared directly with S01/S02. A 2025 report title does not make its data a 2025 survey.
- **Why useful:** Candidate restaurant workflows and a reminder to inspect definitions before quoting adoption rates.
